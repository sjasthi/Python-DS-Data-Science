## 1. Pandas – Concatenation, Merge, and Join

Learn how to combine multiple DataFrames by stacking data, matching common keys, and joining indexes.

### Topics

- Combine DataFrames vertically and horizontally using `pd.concat()`.
- Use `axis` and `ignore_index` when concatenating data.
- Match rows using `pd.merge()` and common key columns.
- Compare inner, left, right, and outer joins.
- Use `DataFrame.join()` and check the combined data for missing values and unexpected rows.
